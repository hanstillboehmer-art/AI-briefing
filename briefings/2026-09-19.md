[Quelle](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_reports/RRA5130-1.html)

[Quelle](https://www.theinsurer.com/ti/news/ai-can-help-insurers-lift-business-volume-but-caution-remains-over-model-design-2026-09-16/)
